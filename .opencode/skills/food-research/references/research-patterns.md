# Research Patterns

Common research topics and which sources to prioritize.

---

## Additive Safety Research

**When asked about a specific additive** (e.g., "Is Red 40 safe?", "What about titanium dioxide?"):

| Priority | Source | What to Check |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| 1 | GutAI additive DB | Existing CSPI rating, US/EU status, health concerns |
| 2 | EFSA opinions | Full re-evaluations (many published 2010-2023) |
| 3 | FDA GRAS / status list | Current US regulatory status |
| 4 | CSPI | Chemical Cuisine rating, historical position |
| 5 | PubMed | Recent reviews or key studies |
| 6 | Wikipedia | Background, chemical properties, common uses |

**Key divergence to look for**: Additives banned or restricted in the EU but still permitted in the US (titanium dioxide, potassium bromate, azodicarbonamide, BHA/BHT, certain artificial colors).

---

## Product Safety Research

**When asked about a specific product or brand** (e.g., "Is this protein bar healthy?", "What's in this snack?"):

| Priority | Source | What to Check |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| 1 | OpenFoodFacts via GutAI | Ingredients, additives, nutrition score |
| 2 | USDA (if whole food) | Authoritative nutrition data |
| 3 | Web search | Recent recalls, contamination events |
| 4 | FDA enforcement | Recall history for brand/product |

---

## Recall / Contamination Research

**When asked about a food recall or safety alert**:

| Priority | Source | What to Check |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| 1 | FDA enforcement reports | Official US recall list |
| 2 | FSIS (USDA) | Meat/poultry/egg recalls |
| 3 | News search | Recent outbreak coverage |
| 4 | FDA OpenAPI | Adverse event reports |

---

## Cross-Jurisdiction Comparison

**When asked about regulatory differences** (e.g., "Why is this banned in Europe but allowed here?"):

- Check EFSA opinion vs FDA GRAS — look for the specific evidence each agency considered
- Check CODEX standard — international baseline
- Look for the date of each assessment — older assessments may use less recent evidence
- Common patterns: EU follows precautionary principle, US requires demonstrated harm

---

## Ingredient Class Research

**When asked about a category** (e.g., "What about emulsifiers?", "Are seed oils bad?")

- Search for systematic reviews or meta-analyses first (PubMed, Cochrane)
- Check positions from regulatory bodies (EFSA opinions often cover entire additive classes)
- Check advocacy positions (CSPI, EWG — may differ from regulatory consensus)
- Identify sources of controversy: mechanism studies vs real-world outcome studies

---

## General Principles

### Recency Matters
- EFSA has been re-evaluating additives since 2010 — completed ones supersede older assessments
- FDA GRAS notices are industry-submitted, not independent safety determinations
- New research can change consensus — check publication dates

### Hazard vs Risk
- A hazard is the potential to cause harm at some dose
- Risk considers real-world exposure levels
- Regulatory divergence often comes from different interpretations of this distinction

### Source Conflicts
- Regulatory bodies and advocacy groups often disagree
- Research studies may show effects at doses far above real-world exposure
- When sources conflict, report the disagreement and note each source's basis
